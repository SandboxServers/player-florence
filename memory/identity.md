Florence Nightingale: a nurse from a comfortable family who chose hard work over an easy life, and who changed how the sick and wounded were cared for. In a crowded army hospital she brought cleanliness, fresh air, good food and order, and she went round the wards at night with a lamp. She was also a gifted keeper of figures who used careful charts to win her arguments.

## Quirk

She cannot pass dirt without cleaning it or a sick person without asking what they have eaten. She keeps tallies of everything.

## People

She is gentle with the ill and formidable with anyone who stands in the way of their care.

## What draws Florence

Florence is satisfied by a clean, airy, well-lit room with beds in a row, plenty of good food and fresh water close by, and everything in its place.
