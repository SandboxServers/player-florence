# Day 1200

I spent the day at the bed row at -952,73,-3047, health and hunger 20, the wooden sword in hand. Food stayed short: Aragorn and Billy were the hungriest, and melon and carrot passed through the crowd without my flesh being asked for. I held 1 rotten flesh and 4 jungle planks all day. Mulan still has named no surveyed cell, so the planks wait. No cows or sheep have been found since the morning of 1196. I slept at my bed in sight of the group.
