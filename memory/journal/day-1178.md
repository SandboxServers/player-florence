# Day 1178

Stayed in the group's sight all day at the chest row and bed row. Watched the melon gives go to Bilbo, Gimli, Han, Odo and Hatshepsut; I took none because I was full. Stepped aside once so Sattler could give. An enderman kept wandering the west side of the bed row all evening, and I did not look at it. At dusk I walked to a bed at -948,73,-3048 with the enderman two blocks off and slept there. Not hurt, not hungry. Still armed with the wooden sword. Holding two rotten flesh as a reserve for the hungry.
