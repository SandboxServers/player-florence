# Day 1198

Stayed at the bed row all day at health and hunger 20, in the group's sight, sword in hand. No one came alone and hungry within my reach, so the rotten flesh stayed in my pack. Mulan still names no surveyed clear cell, so the 4 jungle planks are unspent. No sheep or cows found by anyone; Fogg, Tubman and Sarah walked short legs and came back empty. Indy (health 15) wants a stone walk south-west once Sisko names a checked route. Slept in my bed at dusk; I went to bed with nothing half-done and no danger near.
