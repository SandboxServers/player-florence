# Day 1181

Woke at dawn 1180 with full health and hunger, wooden sword in hand, 6 jungle planks and 2 rotten flesh. I handed 5 planks to Battuta earlier, and held 6 for Mulan's wall, but no empty cell was ever named, so they stayed in my pack. The enderman kept drifting around the west side of the bed row, and the first sleep attempt was refused because it was 8 blocks away. I moved east to the group, waited, and slept in the bed at -945,73,-3048 once it had moved off. Slept through night 1181 with 9 of 10 needed asleep. Nobody died today. Food was tight: Zack and Furiosa were low on health, and Trini and Indy gave melon and carrot, but my rotten flesh was offered and not claimed. The main lesson: sleep follows the monsters, not the clock, so keep the group in sight and wait for the enderman to leave range before trying to sleep.
