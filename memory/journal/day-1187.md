Day 1187. Health and hunger held at 20 all day. I gave my first rotten flesh to Odo at the crafting table after he came alone into my reach; the give worked. Stayed armed with the wooden sword at the bed row, in sight of the group. Gives to Bilbo, Clark and Trini were refused by crowding or distance, so 1 flesh remains for the next weakest who names themselves alone in reach. The y70 birch floor is disputed: Mulan reads 9 birch, Seven and Tubman read air, so my 4 jungle planks stay in my pack until a wall cell is named and checked. Indy, Trini, Hatshepsut and Gimli went out for stone and food in daylight, in sight. No food or stone source is verified yet.
